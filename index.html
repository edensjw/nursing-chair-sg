The one thing that will bite you: PU leather

Three of the listings checked here have "leather peeling" in the description or visible flaking in the photos. Bonded leather and PU break down in Singapore humidity within two to three years and then shed flakes onto the baby. Buy fabric. If the listing says leather, PU or "vegan leather", skip it — even at S$20.

Check it in this order

  • Glide it 20 times. Silent and smooth, or walk away. A grinding track cannot be fixed cheaply.
  • Recline and lock it five times. The catch must hold firmly and release cleanly.
  • Swivel a full turn. Any wobble means a worn bearing.
  • Press hard on both arms and look for cracks where the arm meets the back.
  • Lift every cushion. Smell for sour milk or mildew — the humidity here makes it common.

Before you travel

  • Ask for a photo of the seat front and the arm tops — that is where wear shows first.
  • Ask how many children used it and whether it was stored in an air-conditioned room.
  • Confirm the seller will help carry it to the lift lobby. These are 30–40kg.
  • Book a Lalamove or GoGoX van, roughly S$40–80, before you leave.
  • Pay on collection, after you have sat in it.

Safe use at night

  • Never feed in the chair if you feel drowsy. Falling asleep with a baby in an armchair is linked to a much higher SIDS risk than a cot.
  • If you doze, put the baby down on his back in the cot first.
  • Lock the recline when it is not in use, and once he crawls, keep fingers away from the glide track.
